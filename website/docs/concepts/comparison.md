# Compared to alternatives

There is no single competitor to Bottega Digitale, because Bottega Digitale replaces a *stack* of tools rather than a single product. This page is honest about what each alternative does better and where they fall short for Italian micro-businesses.

## Where the alternatives win

Be honest with yourself:

- **Shopify** is a better pure-play e-commerce engine. If you sell 10,000 SKUs internationally with complex inventory, use Shopify.
- **Treatwell** has a much larger consumer-facing marketplace for beauty bookings. If your traffic strategy is "appear on a marketplace", that's a different product than Bottega Digitale.
- **TeamSystem and Zucchetti** have decades of fiscal-compliance depth across edge cases (split-payment, reverse-charge, intra-EU). Their accountant relationships are unmatched.
- **Fatture in Cloud** is the gold standard for invoicing as a standalone product, and Bottega Digitale will optionally **integrate** with it rather than replace it.

## Where Bottega Digitale wins

- **You only learn one product.** The owner, the staff and the accountant share the same data model.
- **WhatsApp is in the same app as the booking.** No copy-paste between tabs.
- **Templates ship in Italian.** A bakery does not write English-language SEO copy.
- **You can host it yourself.** No platform risk, no foreign data transfer.
- **The free tier is enough to evaluate.** Compare on real bookings, not on a 14-day trial of a half-feature.

## When *not* to choose Bottega Digitale

We'd rather lose a deal than mis-sell. Use something else if:

- You need a complex e-commerce checkout with 1000+ SKUs and B2B price lists.
- You operate in 10 countries and EU residency is not enough.
- Your only need is e-invoicing and you already love Fatture in Cloud — keep it and integrate.
