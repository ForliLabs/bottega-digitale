# Why Bottega Digitale

Italy has **4.7 million companies**. Of those, **94% are microenterprises** — under 10 employees, often a single owner-operator behind a counter. Almost none of them use modern SaaS.

## The problem

Forlì — a typical mid-sized Italian city — was ranked **84th of 108** provincial capitals for digital services. That is the *worst* in Emilia-Romagna. In May 2026, Confartigianato launched a free **"digital check-up"** for SMEs, openly stating *"Il digitale non è il futuro, è l'oggi; nessuna impresa, neppure la più piccola, può rimanere esclusa."*

But the check-up identifies the problem; it doesn't ship a product.

The status quo for an Italian micro-business is:

1. A static website built by a local agency for €1,500 and never updated.
2. WhatsApp messages for bookings, written down on paper.
3. A separate provider (Aruba, Fatture in Cloud) for FatturaPA.
4. Google Business Profile, set up once, abandoned.
5. Zero CRM, zero loyalty, zero analytics.

Each tool is in a different place. None of them speak Italian fiscal compliance natively. None of them are designed for someone running the shop *and* answering the phone *and* serving the customer.

## Why generic tools don't fit

| Tool | What's missing for an Italian SME |
| --- | --- |
| **Wix / Squarespace** | English-first; no FatturaPA; too complex for non-tech users; no bookings or CRM. |
| **Shopify** | Overkill for a bakery; expensive; no e-invoicing; English support; no WhatsApp. |
| **Google Business Profile** | A listing, not a booking tool. No payments, no customer book. |
| **TeamSystem / Zucchetti** | Enterprise-grade, enterprise-priced, enterprise-onboarding. |
| **Facebook / Instagram** | Algorithm-dependent. No booking. No payments. No customer data. |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Useful but unstructured. No scheduling, no analytics, no fiscal docs. |

## What Bottega Digitale changes

Bottega Digitale is opinionated where the others are not:

- **One app, one login.** Bookings, CRM, payments, invoicing and WhatsApp are the same product.
- **Italian fiscal compliance is the floor, not an add-on.** FatturaPA XML, IVA rates, codice fiscale, GDPR audit logs.
- **WhatsApp is the primary channel.** Email is the fallback.
- **Templates beat builders.** Pre-written Italian copy and category templates — bakery, barber, restaurant — let a non-tech owner go live in five minutes.
- **Free tier exists.** A bakery cannot evaluate a €99/month tool. They can evaluate €0.
- **Distribution through associations.** CNA, Confartigianato and Confcommercio reach every SME in Italy by trust. The platform is built to be co-sold.

## What this means for you, the user of these docs

- If you're a **developer**, you get a permissively-shaped reference implementation of multi-tenant Italian SME software — fork it.
- If you're an **integrator**, you get a single API surface to replace the four or five tools your customers cobble together today.
- If you're an **association or commercialista**, you get a white-label dashboard for your members or clients without writing code.
